# Privacy Policy for Drive View

*Last updated: October 26, 2026*

**Drive View** is an open-source Obsidian plugin developed by Alex Sherman. This privacy policy explains how user information is handled when using the plugin.

### 1. Data Access & Storage
- **Google Drive Files:** Drive View requests access to your Google Drive exclusively to read, write, and synchronize your vault notes and folders as requested by you within the Obsidian application.
- **Local Device Storage:** All accessed files, metadata, and authentication tokens are cached and stored locally on your device (using IndexedDB and Obsidian's internal cache).
- **No Third-Party Transmission:** Drive View has no external backend server. Your Google Drive files, notes, and credentials are never sent to, collected by, or stored on any third-party server or database. Network communication occurs exclusively between your device and Google's official APIs (`googleapis.com`).

### 2. Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure
Drive View's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements:
- Google user data is not used for serving advertisements.
- Google user data is not used for training general artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
- Google user data is not transferred or sold to third parties.
