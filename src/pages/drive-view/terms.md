### 1. Description of Software
Drive View is an open-source client-side plugin designed to allow users to mount and synchronize a Google Drive folder as a virtual vault within Obsidian. All network requests occur directly between your device and Google APIs. The Software does not operate, route through, or depend on any intermediate third-party servers.
